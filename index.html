SCORING

Keep hitting bricks before the combo timer runs out to raise your multiplier, up to ×8. Break 3 or more bricks within half a second for a RAPID bonus. Clearing a level pays a clear bonus, 500 per life left, and a perfect bonus if you lost no lives. Every 5th level is a boss.

INSTALL THE APP

Put Zak Breakout on your home screen. It gets its own icon, opens fullscreen and plays without internet.

  1. Tap the Share button in the browser toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Look for the Zak Breakout icon on your home screen.
