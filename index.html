SCORING

Keep hitting bricks before the combo timer runs out to raise your multiplier, up to ×8 (OVERDRIVE). Break 3 or more bricks within half a second for a RAPID bonus. Clearing a level pays a clear bonus, 500 per life left, and a perfect bonus if you lost no lives. Levels 10, 20 and 30 are bosses.

THE JOURNEY

A journey from world to world, in acts that each end with a boss. Clearing a level unlocks the next; after a game over you can start again from any level you have unlocked. Pick EASY, NORMAL or HARD on the journey map: harder means a faster ball and fewer lives, but more points and up to double coins. Each difficulty keeps its own levels and stars.

COINS & GARAGE

Every brick pays a coin. Catch the ball on the white middle of the paddle for a PERFECT, save it at the very last moment for a CLUTCH, and earn more for stars, cleared levels and bosses. Spend coins in the GARAGE on new balls and paddles, on boosts for the level you start your next run from, or on a REVIVE after a game over.
