SCORING

Keep hitting bricks before the combo timer runs out to raise your multiplier, up to ×8 (OVERDRIVE). Break 3 or more bricks within half a second for a RAPID bonus. Clearing a level pays a clear bonus, 500 per life left, and a perfect bonus if you lost no lives. Every 5th level is a boss.

COINS & GARAGE

Every brick pays a coin. Catch the ball on the white middle of the paddle for a PERFECT, save it at the very last moment for a CLUTCH, and earn more for stars, cleared levels and bosses. Spend coins in the GARAGE on new balls and paddles, on boosts for your next run, or on a REVIVE after a game over.
