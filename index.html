SCORING

Keep hitting bricks before the combo timer runs out to raise your multiplier, up to ×8. Break 3 or more bricks within half a second for a RAPID bonus. Clearing a level pays a clear bonus, 500 per life left, and a perfect bonus if you lost no lives. Every 5th level is a boss.
